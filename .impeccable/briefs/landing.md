# AIMDB Landing

## Scope

Public landing page at `/`. Persuade mode. Establishes the visual world for the whole site, including the dense admin surfaces built in later phases.

Audience: UTD students, primarily freshmen and sophomores in the Naveen Jindal School of Management, deciding whether a club they have never heard of is worth joining.

Job: understand what AIMDB is, believe it is legitimate, and register an application.

Action: `/apply`.

Proof: the subject matter itself. No testimonials, no metrics, no officer portraits, no past-event counts exist and none may be invented.

Constraints: brand colors are fixed at `#669DFF`, `#001F54`, `#F0F8FF`. WCAG 2.2 AA. `#669DFF` measures 2.5:1 on `#F0F8FF` and cannot carry text. Banned: dark-neon hacker aesthetic, generic template layout.

## Direction contract

**THESIS**

A working bench log for in-memory systems, not a brochure. The refused arrangement is the centered startup hero: gradient blob, three feature cards, one blue call-to-action. This page is a recording surface — ruled sheets, tabular readings, a bench instrument that is still running. AIMDB proves its seriousness by showing the apparatus, not by claiming outcomes.

**OWN-WORLD**

Ink on `#F0F8FF` sheet stock, the way a lab notebook is actually made. Deep navy `#001F54` carries all text. Hairline rules in a derived navy step divide every band. Tabular figures everywhere a number appears — an instrument does not set numbers in a proportional face. `#669DFF` is ink that cannot be read: it appears only as a bench accent, a live reading, a focus ring, a status mark. Never as body text, never as a text-on-light button label. The equipment is rendered as ruled tables and ruled bands, not as glassmorphic cards. Corners are square to small-radius; an instrument panel does not have rounded friendly cards.

**STORY**

The visitor reads a bench sheet with a live reading on it and understands: this club runs on measurement. The subject matter — AI and in-memory databases — is carried by the instrument's vocabulary, not by a paragraph. A student sees that the apparatus is real, that the club operates like something with standards, and that joining means being handed a bench. One visible primary action, in the vocabulary of the instrument: enroll rather than "Get Started."

**FIRST VIEWPORT**

Top band: navigation as a ruled strip, logotype left, four links right, hairline rule closing it. Below, a two-column arrangement — the left column roughly two-thirds is a ruled bench sheet carrying a live instrument reading with its unit, timestamped and always updating; the right column one-third is a single registration panel, the calmest field on the page, containing the term, the section code, and the single primary action to apply. Below the fold, stacked ruled bands: what the club runs on, what a meeting is, how to join. Density varies deliberately — a dense instrument passage earns a quiet one.

**FORM**

Chosen: bench measurement log. Position in ordered grounded list: first. Seed key `b8c2f066`, assigned index 4, direction card kind `pick`.

**SIGNATURE INTERACTION**

The live reading: one instrument value in the bench sheet that updates on an interval, its last-updated stamp refreshing with it. It is the page's proof of liveness and the reason the design is a log rather than a poster. A second interaction: the enrollment status mark stamps into place on hover of the apply action.

**CROSS-SURFACE REACH**

The admin panel inherits the ruled sheet and tabular figures but inverts to navy ground for long operator sessions. Dues tables are bench ledgers. The public calendar is a log with ruled day bands. Status badges become instrument state marks rather than colored pills.

**HONEST RISK**

A measurement-log world can read austere to a prospective freshman, and the correctness it signals is also a barrier. The single calm registration panel exists to counter this: one obvious door in a precise, orderly page.
